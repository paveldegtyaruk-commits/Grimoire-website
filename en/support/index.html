Grimoire Support

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

Contact

  • Support and bug reports: support@grimoireapp.app.
  • General inquiries: general@grimoireapp.app.
  • Release inquiries: release@grimoireapp.app.

The developer handles all three addresses in a shared mailbox. Send one message to the relevant address. Replies are sent by email; there is no guaranteed response time.

Reporting an issue

Include your Grimoire version, device, operating system version, steps to reproduce, and expected and actual results. Attach a screenshot if needed. You can save an error report to a file from the Info tab in app settings, review it, and attach it to your email. It is not sent automatically and excludes characters, notes, the library, images, and settings. The raw log contains more information; send it only when needed and after reviewing it.

Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or unnecessary personal information. Requests are handled under the Grimoire Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored

Characters, your library, and notes are stored on your device. Grimoire has no accounts or built-in cloud synchronization. Support cannot remotely open or restore your library. Before uninstalling the app or clearing its data, save files you need and check your backups.

Privacy and rights

For correspondence and data processing requests, contact support@grimoireapp.app. To report a possible copyright infringement, identify the material, provide evidence of your rights or authority, and include a way to contact you. Avoid attaching documents containing unnecessary personal details.
