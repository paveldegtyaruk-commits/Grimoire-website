ROADMAP

The next chapter
Grimoire.

Big plans.
One shared story.

Grimoire grew out of our own party’s needs and what we learned at the table. We now want to support more rules, improve the tools you already use, and add new ways to help players and game masters.

Where we’re headed

Grimoire is our first app, and we’re learning as it grows. We work on the project in our spare time, and some steps depend on app store requirements and reviews. We haven’t set dates. The order and scope of these plans may change.

Your feedback helps set our priorities

Tell us what you’d like to see and where you run into difficulties. What you share will help us decide what to work on next.

01TOP PRIORITY

Support for the
2024 rules.

We want to support the revised 2024 D&D rules (5.5e) while keeping the 2014 rules playable.

Grimoire already has flexible editors and configurable mechanics. We want to build on them so you can choose material from either version and combine rules as agreed with your GM.

02PLANNED

Library
illustrations

We plan to add illustrations to library entries for creatures, items, and other game material. The artwork will make entries easier to spot and give the pages more character.

You’ll be able to replace the illustrations with images that suit your character, your party, and your world.

Make the library your own.
03PLANNED

Step-by-step
tutorials

Grimoire covers a lot, from keeping track of a character to building custom classes and their mechanics. We want to make those tools easier to learn.

Step-by-step guides will help you read the character sheet, use abilities and spells, take notes, and create your own material in the editors.

From your first hero to your own mechanics.
04PLANNED

Rules reference
at the table

What counts as a bonus action? How does a saving throw work?

We want to link key terms in the app to short explanations. Tap a term to see what it means, then get back to the game. The reference will help new players get started and give experienced players a quick refresher.

A quick explanation while you play.
05PLANNED

In-game
languages

Grimoire already has the beginnings of a language system. We want to expand it so that the languages your character knows affect how writing appears in their book.

Languages in the library will each get a distinct alphabet. A character who knows the language will see readable text in stylized Cyrillic or Latin letters. For a character who doesn’t, the writing will appear in the language’s own glyphs.

An ancient inscription becomes part of the adventure.

A dedicated GM app is one of our biggest plans.

We envision two companion apps that share game information so everyone can focus on telling the same story.

We want to make prep and running sessions easier, keep the game moving, and make it practical to use mechanics that would otherwise take too much manual tracking.

We’ll share more about what it can do as we develop it.
