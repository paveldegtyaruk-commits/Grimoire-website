01 HOW IT BEGAN

First, for
our own party.

There are many ways to keep track of a D&D character, from small mobile apps made by enthusiasts for their own groups to the official platform D&D Beyond ↗.

Grimoire also began as an app we made for ourselves: a way to solve the problems our own party faced and make playing a little easier. Each new mechanic brought new ideas, and the app grew with them. Over time, a simple digital character sheet became a fully featured companion at the table.

Less bookkeeping.
More of the story.

Our aim is simple: reduce the bookkeeping, keep the game moving and leave more room for the adventure itself — the decisions, conversations and stories that bring us together.

02 AT THE TABLE

Your hero.
And their companions.

Grimoire helps you recalculate stats, including when changing form, track spell slots and ability uses, and keep equipment and active effects in order.

Even your hero’s less frequently used abilities stay within sight. Sometimes one of them is exactly what turns a battle or opens an unexpected path.

If your hero travels with a familiar, a summoned creature or a trusted companion, their stats, abilities and resources will be close at hand too.

03 FREEDOM TO CREATE

Your world.
Your rules.

House rules and original ideas
become part of your grimoire.

Over the decades, D&D has inspired thousands of creatures, items, spells and mechanics. Add house rules, homebrew classes, unusual artifacts and ideas created for a single campaign, and every group gradually builds a world of its own.

No ready-made library can contain all of that. That is why the ability to create and adapt your own game content is central to Grimoire. Its included library was made with the same editors and is open for you to edit.

Imagine.
Configure. Share.

The built-in editors let you add items, spells, creatures, classes and other elements of your game. Use them to describe abilities and configure effects, bonuses, restrictions and connections between entries.

For example, an artifact you invent can have abilities of its own and affect its owner’s stats. The mechanics you configure take part in calculations and appear on the character sheet.

You can share what you create. Grimoire supports importing and exporting individual entries and whole library packs. A character can be transferred along with their notes, images and linked game content.

04 PAGES OF YOUR STORY

The adventure moves on.
The story stays.

Every adventure has a story worth keeping. Grimoire includes a dedicated journal for the events of a session, the names of people you meet, clues and your own theories.

Type, add images and photos, or draw with a stylus, Apple Pencil or your finger. A sketch of a mysterious symbol, a hand-drawn map or a portrait of a suspicious innkeeper can stay right on the pages of your grimoire.

05 ATMOSPHERE AND FREEDOM

A book from
your hero’s world.

Wood. Parchment.
The soft light of a candle.

The design has a special place in this project. An old tome on a wooden stand, textured parchment, brass details and the soft light of a wax candle evoke a book that belongs in your character’s world.

We want the interface to support the atmosphere of an adventure while keeping information easy to find, even in the middle of a battle. Behind the decorated pages are working tools: stats, counters, cards and quick access to your hero’s abilities.

Grimoire is designed primarily for tablets and smaller laptops. These screens let you settle in at the table, open your character’s book and keep everything you need nearby.

◇

Your grimoire stays with you.

Your characters, library and notes are stored on your device. You do not need a Grimoire account or a constant internet connection to play. Continue your adventure wherever your party gathers.
