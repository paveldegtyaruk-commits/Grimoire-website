Grimoire Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

1. Scope and responsibility

This policy explains how data is handled in the Grimoire app, on grimoireapp.app, and when you contact project support.

The app developer and person responsible for handling support requests is Pavel Vadimovich Degtyaruk (Дегтярук Павел Вадимович), Russia. For privacy questions, contact support@grimoireapp.app.

Grimoire is a local companion for creating and managing D&D 5e characters. You do not need a Grimoire account to use the app. The version covered by this policy has no built-in cloud synchronization, advertising modules, user tracking systems, or analytics sent to the developer.

Visiting the website and sending a support email are separate actions. They involve the data processing described below even when you use the app offline.

2. Data within the app

Depending on the features you use, your device may store:

  • characters, game statistics, biographies, inventory, spells, and change history;
  • custom library entries, their names, descriptions, and relationships;
  • text and handwritten notes, added images, portraits, and photographs;
  • interface and app settings, drafts, and local record identifiers;
  • supporting files, intermediate image files, backups, and an error log.

You determine the contents of this data. Character names and game information do not require your real name. If you add personal information, photographs of people, or information about other players, that information is also stored in the app's local files.

The app does not automatically send this data to the developer. The developer has no remote access to your characters, notes, or local database.

3. Camera, images, and files

The camera is used when you choose to take a photo for a note. A selected image or a photograph you take is added to the app's data.

System file selection tools are used to import images, library entries, and other supported materials, and to save exported files. Permissions and available sources depend on your operating system. If you select a file from cloud storage, access to that storage is handled by its provider or your operating system.

Selected files may contain their own metadata, which may be retained when files are copied or exported.

You can change permissions in your device settings. Denying access limits the relevant feature, such as adding a photograph; it does not in itself give the developer access to your device.

The app does not request access to your contacts, microphone, or precise location for its core features.

4. Local diagnostics and technical reports

The app stores an error log on your device to help diagnose failures. It may contain an event identifier, app version, time, error area and type, exception message, technical stack trace, and operation context. The raw log may contain file paths and fragments of user data.

The app also records session state and acknowledgement of error notices locally. An unconfirmed session exit may prompt an offer to save a report after the next launch; this does not establish that a crash caused the exit.

On your action, the app can save a separate technical report as a text file. It contains the date, app version, platform and operating system version, and filtered information about recent errors: time, version, area, type, and permitted technical stack frames. Raw exception messages and context are excluded; local paths in exported stack frames are hidden. Characters, the library, notes, images, and settings are not included in this report.

Neither the log nor the technical report is automatically sent to the developer. You choose where to save the report and whether to email it to support. Review it before sending. If you send the raw log instead of the generated report, it may contain more information, including personal data.

5. Exports and backups

Grimoire lets you save supported materials to files you choose, including library packages, character transfer packages, and PDF character sheets. A character transfer package may include notes, media, and related library entries; review its contents before sharing. An export may contain text, images, and other data you selected or entered.

You decide where to keep exported files and whom to share them with. Sharing a file with another person or saving it to a third-party cloud service happens at your initiative.

The absence of built-in Grimoire cloud synchronization does not disable device backups. Your operating system and services you choose may back up and synchronize data according to their settings and policies. You must also manage those copies through the relevant system or cloud service.

When importing characters, the app may retain a local copy of the source package, previous and prepared character versions, and a recovery journal. These files help protect against interrupted imports and are not sent to the developer.

On Android, the current configuration permits system cloud backup and device transfer for current user storage. App rules exclude logs, temporary library import files, and historical backups. Availability and completeness depend on the OS, settings, provider, and limits; these rules do not guarantee a successful backup. Keep manual exports separately.

6. Local storage and deletion

App data remains on your device while you use the relevant records and files. You can delete unwanted records using the available app controls. Deleting an individual record does not necessarily remove earlier exports, backups, original photographs, or all associated supporting files. Related images may be retained after a library entry is deleted to protect references from characters and backups.

To remove all local app data, use your operating system's app data removal tools or delete the relevant local storage. In portable desktop builds, user files may be located alongside the app and remain after you delete the executable.

Delete exported documents and backups separately wherever you saved them. The developer cannot remotely delete or restore files on your device. Device security, access to the device, and the safekeeping of your backups are under your control.

7. Support correspondence

When you email support@grimoireapp.app, general@grimoireapp.app, or release@grimoireapp.app, we receive your email address, sender name, subject, message contents, attachments, and technical message headers. If you provide your device model, app version, error details, or a screenshot, those details also form part of your request.

We use this information to respond, resolve the reported issue, clarify failures, and protect correspondence against abuse. Where possible, avoid sending information that is unnecessary for resolving your request.

Email delivery and storage are provided by Purelymail, a service operated by Add Rabbit LLC. Correspondence is stored in the service's email infrastructure in the United States and may be processed in the email client we use. The provider's privacy policy is available at https://purelymail.com/privacy.

Requests and attachments are kept until the request is resolved and for no longer than 6 months after the last substantive interaction about it. If a legal obligation or a specific dispute requires longer retention, only the necessary data is kept for the relevant period. Deleted data may remain in the provider's backups until removed under its rules.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of correspondence, contact support@grimoireapp.app. This retention period applies to data managed by the developer; the provider sets its own retention periods for technical logs.

8. Website visits

The grimoireapp.app website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser connects to the hosting servers. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP addresses of visitors to Pages sites for security purposes. GitHub's rules govern its infrastructure's processing of technical information.

The current website pages do not use advertising or analytics systems, registration forms, embedded social widgets, or our own tracking cookies. Fonts and images are served as part of the website's files.

About GitHub Pages: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages.

GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

9. Third-party services and disclosures

Access to correspondence is limited to people who need it to handle the request. HTTPS is used to access webmail and the website. This does not mean that email is end-to-end encrypted or that Grimoire separately encrypts local files. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, or other secrets to support.

We do not sell your personal data or use support correspondence for advertising profiling. Contacting support does not subscribe you to marketing emails.

GitHub and Purelymail are foreign providers used for the website and email. Technical website visit data and correspondence you send may therefore be processed outside Russia and your country of residence. Using these services does not automatically transfer the app's local library to them.

Operating systems, cloud services you use, and app stores independently process data for their own functions, such as app installation, system diagnostics, or backups. Consult the relevant provider's terms for that processing.

Data may also be disclosed when applicable law requires it, limited to what is necessary to meet the requirement.

10. Purposes, legal grounds, and your rights

Local processing provides the app features you choose to use. Support data is used to handle your request, and technical website information is used to provide and secure the website.

The grounds for processing data received by the developer include fulfilling your request or a contract, meeting legal obligations, and other grounds permitted by applicable law. Where separate consent is required for particular processing, it must be obtained before that processing. This policy does not itself replace any legally required consent.

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about the processing of your data, access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing; withdraw consent where processing is based on it; and contact the competent supervisory authority. Send requests to support@grimoireapp.app. Requests are handled within the time limits set by applicable law.

The developer can act only on data available to them, primarily support correspondence. You manage local app files on your device. Requests concerning data independently processed by infrastructure providers may need to be addressed directly to those providers.

11. Minors

Grimoire does not request dates of birth or create age profiles. Where applicable law requires a legal representative's involvement or consent to process a minor's data, those requirements must be followed.

If you believe a support request contains a minor's data that should not be processed, contact support@grimoireapp.app so we can review and delete it unless there is a lawful basis for further retention.

12. Policy changes and contact

This policy is updated when app features, data handling, or service providers change. The document states the date of the new revision. Any separate notice or consent required by law will be handled separately.

Privacy contact: support@grimoireapp.app.

Responsible person: Pavel Vadimovich Degtyaruk (Дегтярук Павел Вадимович), Russia.
